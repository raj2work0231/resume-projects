Enterprise Multi-Source ETL Pipeline Platform

End-to-end extraction and transformation of transactional data from multiple enterprise source systems (SFTP, SharePoint, APIs) into ADLS Gen2 curated Delta tables, orchestrated by Azure Data Factory and Databricks Workflows.

← Back to Portfolio

Solution Overview

This platform ingests transactional data from heterogeneous enterprise sources — SFTP servers (PGP-encrypted), SharePoint document libraries, and internal APIs — standardises it through a Bronze/Silver/Gold pipeline, and delivers curated Delta tables as the single source of truth for operational reporting and near-real-time decision support.
